## Make the first version small

Describe one person, one problem, and one useful outcome. A clear starting point helps separate what needs to work now from what can wait.

## Build the complete path

Even a small product should let someone finish its main task. Pay attention to the empty state, the first action, and the result.

1. Define the problem in a sentence.
2. Build the shortest useful flow.
3. Put it in front of someone who has that problem.
4. Use what you learn to choose the next change.

## Write down the tradeoffs

Every project makes tradeoffs. Keeping a short record of those decisions makes it easier to explain the work and revisit it later.
