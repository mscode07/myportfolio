## Start with the work

A useful post can begin with a small, specific thing: a feature shipped, a decision made, or a problem still being worked through. It does not need to be a finished success story.

Explain the context, show the decision, and share what happened next. The details are what make a story useful to another builder.

## Share the process

A simple structure makes it easier to write consistently:

- What were you trying to build?
- What made it difficult?
- What did you try, and what would you change?

> The unfinished parts can be worth writing about, too.

## Leave room to learn

A personal blog can be a record of evolving ideas. A short reflection on one concrete lesson is often more useful than an exhaustive guide.
